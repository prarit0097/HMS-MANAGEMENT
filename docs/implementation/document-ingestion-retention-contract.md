# Registration ingestion: first-slice retention and recovery ceiling

**DRAFT — 11 November 2026.** Proposed bounded resolution for coordinator reconciliation and independent review; not frozen, implemented, tested or activated. Read with the [persistence](document-ingestion-persistence-contract.md), [sealing](document-ingestion-sealing-contract.md), [runtime](document-ingestion-runtime-contract.md), [API](document-ingestion-api-proposal.md) and [design reconciliation](document-ingestion-design.md) drafts. If approved, this document supersedes their destructive-purge and restoration-execution proposals for the first ingestion slice. S0 source1976cba is already deployed but has no ingestion caller; it does not implement any control below. No code, schema, dependency, tool, live configuration or owner policy changes accompany this document.

## 1. Smallest selected capability

Permit future fresh-installation synthetic intake, ordinary receipt-bound scanning, accepted-file download, authorised status and logical withdrawal only after their complete parent contracts and acceptance gates pass. **Do not implement destructive purge, restore rescan/re-enable, key rewrap or recovery-principal provisioning in this first slice.** There is no purge timer, deletion worker, hold-removal action, recovery route, retry console, operator verdict import or automatic flag flip.

No complete legal/backup hold registry currently exists. Therefore every generation/reservation is retained by default; missing hold data cannot authorise deletion. The earlier two-check/24hour orphan narrative and proposed seven-day minimum are not deletion permission or an adopted hospital retention period. Do not invent retention law or make staff choose a retention policy during upload. A later separately authorised contract must establish applicable owner policy, hold sources, precedence, audit and deletion authority before destructive capabilities exist.

## 2. Exact retained states and accounting

Keep the agreed reservation grammar for forward compatibility, but enable only `reserved → sealing → bound`, or either unbound state to `held_terminal`. First-slice services reject transitions to `purging` or `purged`; no callable path sets `released_at`. If those states appear unexpectedly in a first-slice installation, close intake and preserve the rows/objects for reconciliation, rather than interpreting them as permission to release capacity. Proposed GC-check/hold-revision fields supply no authority and can remain deferred with the destructive workflow.

An interrupted used sealing identity, expired unbound final, malformed/partial object, unresolved write/commit or rejected generation is retained. A complete final may still bind under the sealing contract's original cutoff and fresh authority; this is reconciliation, not replacement or purge. Once binding eligibility has ended, its declaration is permanently fenced and projects unavailable; the staff next step is an explicit new declaration. Unknown ACK is never proof of rollback or absence.

Retain all referenced document KEK versions and wrapped-DEK metadata, including terminal/orphan generations and backup dependencies. No automatic key retirement, replacement of a version's key bytes or rewrap exists. Exact retained-key lookup remains separate from eligibility for new sealing; missing keys deny affected use and preserve evidence, never justify deletion. This makes no change to existing MFA keys.

The proposed logical storage charge is `4096 + 4096 * ceil((declared_size + 78 + 16) / 4096)`. New reservation increments reserved bytes; known binding moves exactly that charge from reserved to retained without changing the total. `held_terminal` leaves it reserved. Withdrawn, rejected, unavailable and accepted bound objects remain retained and charged. **No storage refund occurs**, including a reservation that received no bytes: deliberately conservative admission is preferable to an uncontracted release path. Counter reconciliation may detect discrepancies and close intake; it cannot lower charges or delete objects to recover capacity.

Transfer slots are separate from retained storage. A known completed transfer, or supervisor-confirmed termination of its exact writer and descendants, permits one conditional UUID-token/writer-instance slot release and decrement. Lease expiry, client disconnect, withdrawal or process silence alone does not. Unknown release ACK is resolved through durable markers; never double-decrement. DB/ownership uncertainty retains the slot and refuses work at capacity. No storage/scanner/process I/O occurs under business locks.

Full storage/queue or lost intake readiness refuses new first reservations. It does not hide already accepted content, stop otherwise eligible status/withdrawal, erase retained evidence or force a new accepted verdict. A committed receive replay uses bounded transfer/rate capacity but allocates no new generation/storage charge; download likewise requires its transfer slot, current reader authority, same-epoch clearance, integrity and committed audit. Dependency or integrity failure denies affected disclosure without altering historical acceptance.

## 3. Withdrawal and ordinary promotion

Withdrawal is durable visibility denial, not deletion or erasure. Old parent-intent withdrawal and new content withdrawal both override new download/promotion; preserve content, envelope, key reference, work, attempts, receipts, audit and accounting. No automatic retention clock starts a purge. Existing creator/grant/version rules and API traceable corrections remain required.

Ordinary work has purpose `ingest`, the receiving installation epoch and original receiver/epoch/origin provenance. Its ten-minute deadline starts at original receipt, with at most three issued ordinals and no reset. Claim/promotion rechecks current actor active/epoch, exact branch-conjoined grants, current recorded-origin IP policy, withdrawal/security denial and matching supervisor/readiness/lease identities. Ordinary logout alone does not cancel committed scan work. Successful promotion writes immutable acceptance and current-epoch clearance atomically with audit; it grants no clinical-signing authority.

No first-slice worker claims purpose `restore`, adopts copied work, changes receipt time or creates a fresh scan cycle to rescue expired/restored work. Existing future-compatible restore fields are not permission. Restored files cannot become downloadable merely because their copied historical verdict says accepted.

## 4. Durable guard outside restored snapshots

Propose one root-controlled guard at fixed `/var/lib/hms-ingest-control/epoch.json`, outside the DB/object restore namespace, directory0750/file0640, root ownership and the dedicated `hms-ingest-guard-read` group, nonsymlink ancestry and no application write access. Version1 is a closed object: `version:1`, canonical UUID strings `installation_id` and `epoch`, `mode:active|closed`, `reason:fresh|maintenance|restore`, finite UTC `recorded_at`, and `manifest_sha256:null|64-lowercase-hex`. Fresh initialisation records a new installation/epoch, starts closed and cannot be auto-created by requests or workers. This is a proposed local installation control, not a delivered recovery tool.

Future reviewed installation activation may set active only for a demonstrably fresh namespace under the same epoch after acceptance. Maintenance on an unchanged namespace may reopen only through reviewed operational preservation checks. A guard marked `restore`, missing/corrupt guard, epoch mismatch or unknown database provenance has **no first-slice reopen path**. No environment default overrides it. All new ingestion admission/readiness/claim/promotion/final-download checks require a valid active guard matching Control's epoch and installation identity. The persistence contract must add immutable `installation_id` to Control; it is not currently supplied by its epoch field alone. Routine service restart never generates a new epoch or resets counters.

Before any future restoration procedure replaces DB/object state: keep the application/bytes closed, stop and fence owned workers, then durably write a fresh target epoch with mode closed/reason restore and selected manifest hash. Write a new exclusive private temp file, fsync it, atomically replace the fixed guard and fsync its directory; uncertain persistence means remain closed. Guard change is independently retained in private operational receipts. Restoring old DB flags, guard copies from backup or old leases cannot replace this external current guard. DB/object backup manifests record the observed guard identity for diagnosis, never instruct restoration to overwrite the current guard.

Restoration may be rehearsed offline to verify keys/manifests/retained history. It may not start an ingestion worker or byte-serving process. A future separately reviewed recovery workflow must install the new target identity in restored Control with gates/readiness false, reconcile all old workers/clearances and establish scoped current recovery authority before reconsidering enablement. That workflow/principal/hold schema is intentionally absent here. Root database replacement outside the guarded procedure cannot be reliably detected by this contract; it is not an automatic anti-rollback guarantee against an administrator bypassing maintenance.

### Reader mechanism and freshness — coordinator correction, still DRAFT

The narrowly scoped readonly group contains only the application discovery/admission service UID `hms-preview`, byte UID `hms-ingest-bytes`, worker UID `hms-ingest-work` and supervisor UID `hms-ingest-avctl`. It conveys no content/key/scanner/broker/DB privilege; scanner, decoder, updater, UI/MCP clients and other apps are excluded. This is the sole exception to the runtime draft's no shared supplementary-group rule. Root alone creates/replaces the guard and changes group membership; readers cannot create/delete/replace/chmod it. Guard identities are control metadata, not secret key material. No group or permission is provisioned by this draft.

Each mandatory check opens the current fixed basename afresh relative to a verified trusted directory descriptor, with no-follow/close-on-exec, regular-file/root-owner/exact-group/mode/no-extra-ACL checks. Read at most4097bytes and reject more than4096, malformed/duplicate/extra fields, noncanonical UUIDs or invalid state. Never reuse a prior inode, startup cache, caller-supplied value or successful permission fallback. Missing/unreadable/corrupt/mismatching guard fails closed; discovery may only report unavailable. Timestamp is provenance, not permission to cache it. The exact codec and OS acceptance tests remain unfrozen.

Checks establish current selection boundaries, not instantaneous recall of selected bytes or atomic filesystem-plus-DB COMMIT. Restoration still requires maintenance closure and confirmed cessation of every owned byte/worker process before replacing state. An active-to-closed race cannot authorise continued work across that guarded replacement. Arbitrary administrator bypass remains explicitly undetectable.

## 5. Capacity and remaining gates

The 1GiB synthetic object/accounting cap, twenty queued generations per organisation, actor sixty calls per UTC minute, transfer slots and runtime budgets remain proposed bounded test parameters. They are **not clinically validated for555+OPD/day**, future branches, peak arrivals, images per visit or retention duration. Retain-all deliberately exhausts a finite cap eventually; the response is refused new intake plus operator capacity notice, never eviction, silent cap growth, quota refund or a claim that production volume is supported. No paid storage, new host or other-application resource change is selected.

Technical gates before any parent freeze: align state constraints/projections/counters and installation identity; independent scenario/threat/test review; prove guard corruption/missing/old-snapshot mismatch and close-before-restore ordering; actual PostgreSQL races/unknown ACK/slot accounting; real sealing/storage/scanner/isolation and complete backup+DB+object+key integrity rehearsals; exact API/page-action contracts. Tests must prove zero unlink/purge/rewrap/restore-claim paths, logical withdrawal retains all bytes/history/charges, and cap exhaustion leaves accepted download/status/withdrawal available when their independent prerequisites hold.

Owner-policy gates for production remain explicit: approved retention/hold/legal obligations, durable capacity and growth plan for representative hospital workload, supported backup/DR and a tested scoped recovery/re-enable capability. Deferring deletion removes an unsafe feature; it does not make an ingestion service without recovery ready for real hospital records. Browser/staff/phone/full-release acceptance remains separate. This author ran no tests and made no live change.
