# ICTHospital comparison with the Circadian HMS plan

Reviewed: 24 October 2026, Asia/Calcutta. Status: source research and planning input; **no imported code, installed package, application execution, feature implementation or deployment**.

## Decision

Keep the Django/DRF, React/TypeScript and PostgreSQL architecture. Use ICTHospital as a reference for hospital questions and workflows. Most of its useful concepts already appear in our plan; none justifies a framework/database switch. Record laboratory as an explicit future design candidate, and refine the reporting/reminder backlog without silently adding release scope. This is an engineering recommendation under the owner's request to assess useful features, not approval of a new laboratory release.

## Architecture, maintenance and licence

The inspected Composer manifest requires PHP^8.2 and Laravel^12.61; its [lockfile pins Laravel v12.69.2](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/composer.lock#L1778). The repository description still says Laravel11; do not use that label as dependency evidence. Hospital workflows are server-rendered PHP/Blade actions; the API route file exposes an authenticated user endpoint rather than a complete versioned hospital API. Converting that application would require substantial contract/service/client work. Model names or marketing text do not establish a working payroll, theatre, blood-bank, imaging or teleconsultation portal. ### Reporting questions worth preserving, formulas requiring fresh design

All are G01/G02/Task16 planning refinements, not implemented reports. The laboratory report remains dependent on a future laboratory scope decision.

| Question | Source observation | Required HMS definition |
|---|---|---|
| Which tests remain pending, and how old are they? | Per-test backlog uses a fixed three-day overdue flag | Agree test-specific SLA, start clock, pauses and clinical priority |
| What was ordered versus supplied? | Prescription-line count is compared with dispensed quantity, grouped by name | Compare source-linked quantities in compatible units, account for cancellation/partial supply/returns; do not call unlike denominators a fulfilment rate |
| Which receivables are overdue? | Entire patient outstanding amount uses the oldest invoice's bucket | Age remaining invoice/receivable allocations individually, with agreed date basis and payer responsibility |
| What is occupancy and length of stay? | Daily count includes admissions touching a day | Define census instant or occupied bed-time and historical usable capacity; same-day turnover must not inflate occupancy. Define stay cohort and day rounding separately from the upstream minimum-one-day convention for closed stays overlapping the period |

Source: [ReportController.php](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/ReportController.php#L56-L235). These cautions are static formula observations, not validation of local report output.

## Security and integrity patterns

Useful concepts to implement independently: child rows instead of packed repeated fields; transactional stock checks with duplicate-line aggregation; invoice parent locking before balance checks; negative permission-route tests. Actual PostgreSQL contention, revisions, idempotency and service-level reauthorisation remain mandatory in our implementation.

Patterns that cannot be adopted directly:

- The inspected role-permission and patient model do not establish organisation/branch/data-category isolation equivalent to our requirements. A global Admin grant is not our master-admin clinical ceiling. [Permission middleware](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Middleware/CheckPermission.php), [Patient model](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Models/Patient.php).
- Medical-history uploads use public-disk delivery paths. Our clinical attachments must be private, validated/quarantined and permission-checked on retrieval, not protected only by an unguessable name. [History controller](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/MedicalHistoryController.php).
- The reviewed verification-code login path needs expiry/consumption/session-binding controls before it could meet our identity contract. Our existing encrypted TOTP/recovery/epoch/freshness design is not replaced by it. [Users controller](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/UsersController.php).
- The inspected pharmacy reversal path reads items before its transaction and lacks a locked one-time reversal claim before restocking/deletion. We require traceable movement/reversal history and concurrent retry tests rather than copying that path. [Pharmacy controller](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/PharmacyController.php).
- Matching occupied-bed/overlapping-appointment locks do not establish portable protection when the result set is empty. Preserve stable resource locks/constraints and actual PostgreSQL interleavings in our design. [ConcurrentWriteTest](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/tests/Feature/ConcurrentWriteTest.php#L8) expressly limits its SQLite evidence.
- A public GET reminder route is declared outside the authenticated hospital route group; configured execution can cause external side effects. Our job capabilities require explicit authority, audit and duplicate-delivery controls. No messages were sent. [Reminder route](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/routes/web.php#L278), [controller](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/CronjobController.php#L118).
- Legacy media code combines a client-extension-derived filename with an unquoted shell command. Do not reuse that execution boundary. Reachability and exploitability were not tested. [Media controller](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/ICTCoreController.php#L182).
- Logger tooling includes clear/delete functions; its presence does not prove immutable business audit. Our append/correction/read-audit design and retention remain separate gates. [Logger controller](https://github.com/ictinnovations/ICTHospital/blob/e506dfb553f7d146a6d0cddc136ffd5b6c9bdb09/app/Http/Controllers/LaravelLoggerController.php#L156).

These are bounded source-review concerns, not executed exploit findings or claims that an arbitrary deployed ICTHospital instance is compromised. No assurance of our own full product security follows from identifying them. Full network management, protected files, clinical/financial integrity, recovery, mixed-load and real staff acceptance remain our release gates.

## Backlog and acceptance before adoption

1. Preserve the existing API-first stack and Task03 priority; no imported runtime or framework migration.
2. Record laboratory as a deferred candidate only. A later release decision must choose in-house, external-lab handoff or neither, then freeze its own pages/APIs/permissions/failure/acceptance design. Do not block present hospital work on this choice.
3. During Task16 design, define the three non-lab report questions above with auditable denominators, source links, branch permissions, date/currency/unit handling and bounded freshness. Tests include same-day bed turnover, invoices with different due dates, partial supply and returns.
4. During Tasks05/11 design, make catalogue snapshots and signed/final-record corrections explicit in contracts and regression cases; do not duplicate live catalogue text as the sole clinical identity.
5. Keep outbound reminders unconfigured until consent, data disclosure, provider/cost and deduplicated delivery contracts are reviewed. Existing operational follow-up does not imply permission to send SMS or integrate a call center.
6. Any later source/assets reuse requires a recorded licence/provenance decision and fresh supply-chain/security review. This research adds no external dependency, API operation, migration or live configuration.

Verification for this change: source/plan comparison, independent bounded specialist reviews, upstream CI job/log inspection and local living-document/link/API-map/staged-secret checks.
